Standard eBike Classes

Most US states recognize the three-tier classification system, with some states now considering a Class 4 designation.

Class 1
Pedal Assist

Motor engages only while pedaling. No throttle.

Max: 20 MPH · 750W
Class 2
Throttle Assist

Can be propelled by motor alone without pedaling.

Max: 20 MPH · 750W
Class 3
High-Speed Pedal Assist

Pedal assist only, up to 28 MPH. Usually restricted to riders 16+.

Max: 28 MPH · 750W
Out-of-Class
eMoto / "Class 4"

Often treated as mopeds or off-highway vehicles.

> 28 MPH or > 750W

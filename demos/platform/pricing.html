Common Questions

What industries does this work for?

Any industry with products that benefit from structured comparison data — eBikes, RVs, marine, ATVs, power tools, industrial equipment, real estate, and more. The platform is content-agnostic.

What's included in the setup fee?

Initial data ingestion, brand configuration (colors, domain, logo), deployment of your comparison tool, and onboarding support. Pro includes dedicated white-glove onboarding with a project manager.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Yes. You can upgrade at any time and we'll prorate the difference. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.

Do you offer annual pricing?

Yes — annual contracts receive a 5% discount off the monthly rate, billed as a single upfront payment. Switch using the toggle above the plans.

What does the client portal give my team?

Your team gets visibility into deployment status, running features, project milestones, invoices, and a direct message channel with the Aeglos team.
